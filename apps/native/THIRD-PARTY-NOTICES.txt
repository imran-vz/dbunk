Native additions: Table Seeding data

Table Seeding generates test data from data compiled into the app. Sources
and generation are described in backend/data/seed/MANIFEST.md.

- Postcodes, localities and regions: GeoNames (https://www.geonames.org/),
  licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 License
  (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The data was filtered and
  sampled.
- Address formats, postcode patterns and subdivision codes: Google
  libaddressinput, Copyright (C) 2013 Google Inc., licensed under the Apache
  License, Version 2.0 (https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0).
- Phone number metadata: Google libphonenumber, Copyright (C) 2009 The
  Libphonenumber Authors, licensed under the Apache License, Version 2.0, as
  bundled by the phonenumber crate.
- Names and Japanese municipality names: Wikidata, CC0 1.0. US family names:
  US Census Bureau, public domain. Prose: public-domain novels.
- Fiction-only phone ranges as published by NANPA, Ofcom, the
  Bundesnetzagentur (Mitteilung 148/2021) and ARCEP (Décision 2018-0881).
