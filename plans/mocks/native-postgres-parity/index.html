Required states in every direction

Write refused

Read-only connection. No statement sent. Keep pending edits available to inspect or discard.

Row changed elsewhere

Original values no longer match. Keep the draft, show the conflict, and require an explicit refresh/review.

Connection lost

Results are stale. Reconnect explicitly. Never replay a mutation or restore a transaction.

Unsaved workspace

Keep SQL in memory and the last durable snapshot. Retry, export SQL, or explicitly discard before closing.
